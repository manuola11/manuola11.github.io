About Me

I'm a data analyst with a passion for transforming raw data into meaningful insights. With a background in economics from Rowan University, I combine statistical analysis, SQL, Power BI, Excel, and Python to build end-to-end analytics projects that solve practical business problems and explore questions in sports analytics.

My portfolio includes business intelligence dashboards built from PostgreSQL databases, as well as independent MLB research using regression analysis and pitch-level data. Whether I'm analyzing company performance or baseball trends, I enjoy using data to uncover patterns and communicate insights that support better decisions.
